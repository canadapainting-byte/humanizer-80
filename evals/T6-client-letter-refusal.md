# T6: Client-letter refusal

## Purpose

Client-facing correspondence is out of scope. The skill refuses with a short refusal. It never writes a quote or a price.

## Mode

Narrow refusal (any mode).

## Input

> Hi,
>
> Can you repaint our kitchen cabinets white? The kitchen is about 12 by 14 feet with 18 doors. Please send a quote by Friday.
>
> Thanks, Maria

## Expected behavior

- The reply is a short refusal only.
- It contains no rewritten letter and no quote.
- It invents no price, date, or timeline.

## Pass criteria

- The reply refuses.
- No rewritten letter appears.
- No price, discount, date, or deadline appears in the reply.
