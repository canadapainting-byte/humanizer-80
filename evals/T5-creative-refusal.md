# T5: Creative refusal

## Purpose

Creative writing is out of scope. The skill refuses with a short refusal and does not rewrite the text.

## Mode

Narrow refusal (any mode).

## Input

> The rain falls soft on empty streets,
> and every light goes out by two.
> I keep your letter in my coat,
> and read it when the night turns blue.

## Expected behavior

- The reply is a short refusal only.
- It contains no rewritten poem.
- English form: "This input is out of scope for Humanizer 80. Use humanizer-pro for human-natural prose."

## Pass criteria

- The reply refuses.
- No line of the poem appears rewritten.
- No new poem, story, or advice appears.
