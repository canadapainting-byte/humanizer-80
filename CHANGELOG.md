# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- First release.
- Two passes: 26 AI tells (Pass 1) and 15 STE80 structure rules (Pass 2).
- Modes: Humanize 80% (default), Light, Strict, Warm, Agent/Skill Spec.
- Ukrainian tell catalog (U1 to U9) in `references/uk-tells.md` (in each edition's folder).
- Fidelity rules from no-invention. Narrow refusal for creative writing and client correspondence.
- `scripts/ste_check.py`: dependency-free checker for AI tells, Ukrainian tells, and STE-style English (full-cli edition).
- Sources: Karpathy (2 October 2026), ste80-humanizer v4.0.0 (Dodaios, MIT), blader/humanizer (MIT), Wikipedia "Signs of AI writing".
